# Code of Conduct

Be kind, direct, and constructive. We follow the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):

- Respectful, inclusive language; no harassment or personal attacks.
- Focus criticism on the work, with actionable suggestions.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior may lead to removal from the project space.
Reports are handled privately and promptly by the maintainers.
